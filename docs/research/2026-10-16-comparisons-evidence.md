# MeetOnTrack comparison evidence

Checked 2026-10-16. Canonical facts and citations: `docs/compare/content.json`.
Public pages are generated by `node scripts/build-comparisons.cjs`.

## Cross-check

Host: GPT-6.1 Sol. Independent web checks: Claude Opus 5.5
(`claude-opus-5-5`) and Gemini 3.8 Flash High
(`gemini-3.8-flash-high` via agy). Both actually ran and reported web access.
Same-family GPT was not counted as independent. This follows check-skill-solo.

- Confirmed Granola's bot-free capture, external transcription/AI providers and
  absence of a retained replayable audio archive. Preserved mobile temporary
  audio-cache caveat. Full-product OSS rights were not positively verified,
  so the matrix does not categorically assert their absence.
- Confirmed Fireflies has bot and system-audio capture, with Live Assist in
  both. Its desktop guide distinguishes retained media and named speaker
  attribution by mode. Numbered speakers are not described as no labels at all.
- Confirmed Bluedot bot-free capture, Mac/Windows availability, AWS storage and
  five-lifetime-meetings free tier. No claim that bot-free is its only mode.
- Confirmed tl;dv's newer bot-free desktop route, cloud hosting and
  plan-dependent multilingual switching. Vendor pages conflict on native mobile
  apps, so availability is explicitly unverified. Its own review is identified
  as vendor documentation, not an independent benchmark.
- API, SDK, MCP and open-source dependencies are not treated as licensing the
  complete application. Fireflies/Bluedot/tl;dv service terms grant proprietary
  service access rather than MIT-style application redistribution rights.

Mechanical readback with `node scripts/check-comparison-sources.cjs` returned
HTTP 200 for all 24 retained URLs and matched all configured load-bearing
passages on 2026-10-16 at 16:42 UTC. An HTTP success alone does not establish
every product claim. The source checker tests selected exact passages too.

## Publishing boundaries

Compare pinned Mac v0.1.9 and Windows v0.1.4 packages, not local unreleased
changes. Do not call the public beta fully offline, notarized or automatically
updating. No accuracy ranking, invented subscription fee or Git-search absence
claim is published. Price amounts are deliberately not quoted across billing
periods. The article links to live pricing and discloses plan dependence.

This is public-document research, not authenticated competitor-service testing.
No private records, credentials or transcripts were used. Development-only
design/review records are not included in the Pages artifact.
