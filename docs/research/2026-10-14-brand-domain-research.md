# Meeting Copilot brand and domain research

Research date: 2026-10-14. Decision status: provisional shortlist, not an approved rebrand.
No domain was purchased, and no installed application, public website or release was changed.

## Recommendation

**MeetOnTrack** / **meetontrack.com** is the provisional first choice.

“Meet” signals meetings; “on track” conveys keeping a conversation focused and
turning it into questions, risks, decisions and next steps. It covers the live
assistant and post-meeting record without limiting the product to transcription.
Suggested Russian pronunciation: “Мит-он-трэк”.

This is a naming judgment, not evidence of customer preference. It is more
explanatory than an invented word but less ownable than a strongly coined brand.
“OnTrack” is a common expression; trademark clearance is still required.

Suggested category descriptor:

- English: **MeetOnTrack — your AI meeting assistant.**
- English supporting line: **Record the conversation. See questions, risks and decisions as it unfolds.**
- Russian: **MeetOnTrack — ИИ-помощник для встреч.**
- Russian supporting line: **Запись и расшифровка, вопросы, риски и решения — во время разговора и после него.**

The positioning follows current product source: desktop capture, live transcript,
a call map, contextual assistance and reports. Do not promise that absolutely
nothing leaves the computer: external AI services are optional, and configured
providers can receive dialogue or selected context.

## Shortlist and tradeoffs

1. **MeetOnTrack / meetontrack.com.** First choice for understandable English
   positioning. Longer than a coined brand; “on track” requires more explanation
   for Russian-only users. Limited exact-name web screening found no clear
   identical meeting-assistant product; this is not a trademark clearance.
2. **MeetInContext / meetincontext.com.** Best descriptive alternative for the
   differentiator: conversation plus relevant context. Understandable, but long
   and less catchy. “Meet in context” is also descriptive language used by other
   software products, so exclusivity should not be assumed.
3. **MeetTact / meettact.com.** Shortest and most brand-like: “Meet + tact,” with
   tactical/supportive associations. Do not choose without an additional
   confusion review: [Tactiq](https://tactiq.io/) operates in AI meeting notes.
   This is a phonetic/category proximity concern, not a finding of infringement.

**CallInContext / callincontext.com** is an available descriptive backup; it is
long and narrows the impression to calls rather than meetings and webinars.

At 18:59:35 UTC on 2026-10-14, Namecheap explicitly reported all four domains
available. Each had a USD 10.98 purchase quote. The API did not specify the term,
renewal, taxes or additional fees: this is not a full annual-cost estimate.
Subsequent authoritative Verisign .com RDAP checks returned HTTP 404 for all four.
Registrar availability is time-sensitive; repeat the check immediately before purchase.

Evidence: [registrar and registry checks](2026-10-14-domain-checks.json).

## Names screened out

Domain availability alone did not determine the shortlist.

- **MeetCue:** [an existing meeting teleprompter](https://www.getmeetcue.app/).
- **MeetHints / CallHints:** their .com domains were reported available, but
  [Hints](https://hints.so/) is an adjacent AI CRM assistant brand. An indexed
  call-capturing landing page was found, but that specific URL now returns 404;
  its historical description is not evidence of a currently offered feature.
- **MeetTactic:** possible proximity to Tactiq and
  [Tactic workplace software](https://www.gettactic.com/).
- **MeetNotum / MeetRecado / MeetCordo:** search discovered adjacent or direct
  products using the roots:
  [Notum AI Note Taker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/notum-ai-note-taker/id6757533461),
  [Recado](https://olevior.com/), [Cordo](https://cordoai.com/).
  These were search-discovery exclusions; not all pages had an independent live
  content verification.
- **MeetClarifier / CallClarifier:** available domains, but cumbersome names.
- **MeetOnPoint / MeetInFlow / MeetOnCue:** .com domains reported unavailable;
  do not build a naming choice around adding “get” to someone else's category name.

This was a web/domain screen, not a comprehensive company, app-store, social-handle
or trademark-register search. It cannot establish worldwide legal availability.

## SEO: candidate semantic core, not measured search volumes

The market/region has not yet been selected by the owner. No Wordstat region was
assumed. No verified keyword volumes, difficulty scores or traffic forecasts were
obtained. The following are candidate intent clusters, not a demand ranking.

English category language is corroborated by primary product pages:
[Krisp](https://krisp.ai/ai-meeting-assistant/) uses meeting assistant,
transcription, recording and summary categories;
[Tactiq](https://tactiq.io/) uses AI note taker.

- **AI meeting assistant:** homepage category; broader than notetaking.
- **AI note taker / AI meeting notes:** notes and outcomes use-case page.
- **Meeting transcription / meeting recording / meeting summary:** separate
  feature pages with actual supported capabilities and workflow demonstrations.
- **Real-time AI meeting assistant:** live questions, risks and decisions.
- **Bot-free meeting recorder:** capture without adding a meeting participant.
- **Local / offline meeting transcription:** local mode and its actual hardware,
  model and feature limitations. Do not conflate local capture with fully offline AI.
- **Meeting assistant for Mac / Windows:** platform pages, with installation and
  release maturity stated accurately.

Russian candidate clusters:

- “расшифровка встреч”, “расшифровка созвонов”;
- “протокол встречи”, “протокол совещания”;
- “запись звонков”, “запись встречи”;
- “ИИ-помощник для встреч”;
- platform-specific “расшифровка Zoom”, “расшифровка Google Meet”;
- “локальная расшифровка”, “запись встреч без бота”.

Category wording is also visible in primary Russian pages:
[Speech2Text](https://speech2text.ru/online-Rasshifrovka-vstrech-v-GoogleMeet)
and [НаВстрече](https://navstreche.com/protocol).
These pages corroborate vocabulary, not search frequency.

Once geography is confirmed, measure these clusters and variants in the selected
markets before choosing the first SEO pages. Use actual volumes and search intent;
do not convert third-party landing-page sample data into research findings.

## Domain keywords and .com

A descriptive keyword is useful for comprehension, but is not an automatic SEO
shortcut. Google's [ranking systems guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ranking-systems-guide)
describes domain words as one relevance input and an exact-match system that
reduces excessive credit for domains matching queries.

Google's [site-position FAQ](https://developers.google.com/search/help/site-position-in-search-faq)
says the top-level domain itself does not change performance.
The .com preference here is a branding/usability judgment, not a promised ranking boost.

Use the chosen brand consistently, with “AI meeting assistant” in the category
descriptor and meaningful feature/use-case pages. Avoid a keyword-stuffed brand,
unsupported “best” claims, or publishing multiple competing brand names.

## Verification and limitations

- Search discovery: Keenable first; a second search index was used for
  consequential collision checks. Sparse exact-name results are not proof of absence.
- Product scope: current repository README, not the obsolete Granola-based setup.
- Final availability: fresh Namecheap check plus authoritative .com RDAP response.
- Source mechanics: live HTML checked for Google SEO passages, Krisp category
  terms, Tactiq note-taker wording, MeetCue teleprompter wording, Hints CRM wording,
  and the Russian transcription/protocol terms.
- The required check-skill-solo independent-model verification degraded:
  Gemini via agy / gemini-3.8-flash-high timed out after 100 seconds without a
  usable verdict. Claude / claude-sonnet-4-6 refused inference because of its
  current context/usage-credit limit, including a retry without settings sources.
  Neither returned agreement or a fact-check verdict. No credits were enabled.
  GPT would be the host family and was not counted as independent.
- Remaining gates: owner naming preference, market/region selection for measured
  keyword work, and professional trademark/confusion review before adopting a name.
