Open source is not a free-plan checkbox

For MeetOnTrack, the public MIT licence gives you rights to inspect, modify and redistribute the application, subject to its terms and third-party notices. That does not make Codex or another connected AI provider open source, and it does not make every feature work offline.

For each alternative, we distinguish the complete product's licence from public SDKs, APIs, MCP connections and open-source dependencies. Where full-product source rights cannot be verified, the page says so rather than inventing a negative.

The criteria

  • Open source and licence
  • Bot or device capture
  • Platforms and installation
  • Local processing and offline use
  • Can you keep the audio?
  • Context beyond the transcript
  • Help during the call
  • Languages and switching mid-call
  • Who said what
  • Exports and connections
  • Cost and dependencies
  • Setup and rollout trade-off

Compare the package you can actually install

The pinned packages are Mac v0.1.9 and Windows v0.1.4. They have different requirements and limitations. The source tree can be newer than either package. Development previews on the homepage do not extend the public beta's feature scope.

Automatic multilingual recognition, model choices, update notifications and newer integrations must be checked against the exact installed build. We do not claim tested parity between operating systems, guaranteed speaker attribution, fully offline AI or enterprise compliance certification.

How this comparison was made

Published by AI Agent Labs, maker of MeetOnTrack. This is a vendor-authored comparison, not an independent accuracy benchmark. Competitor capabilities come from official product, help, pricing and policy pages, checked on the dates shown on the individual comparisons. We did not run comparative performance tests.

We use the same criteria for every product. An undocumented feature is marked unverified, not absent. Device capture, on-device speech recognition, cloud storage and cloud AI are separate questions. A free plan, public SDK or MCP endpoint is not an open-source licence for the whole application.

Check current plans on the product’s homepage. Plan limits and capture modes can change. Obtain the required participant consent regardless of whether a bot appears in the meeting.
